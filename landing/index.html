Questions

Do I need to download an app?

No. Pawsie runs in your phone's browser. You can add it to your Home Screen. On iPhone, alerts only work once it's added to the Home Screen.

Does my vet need to do anything?

No. Your clinic doesn't need to set anything up. You just photograph the paperwork you were given.

What if it reads something wrong?

That's why you confirm each medicine against its bottle before the plan starts. Anything that doesn't match is flagged first. When in doubt, call your clinic.

Can the whole family use one plan?

Yes. Share a link and everyone sees the same schedule. Doses marked given, skipped or as-needed sync between phones.

What animals does it work for?

Any pet that comes home with a discharge sheet and medicines. Dogs and cats are the most common.

Is it a replacement for veterinary advice?

No. It's an organizer for the instructions your vet already gave you. For medical questions, call your clinic or an emergency vet.

Are you a vet clinic?

Recommend Pawsie at discharge. Your team does no extra work, and owners go home with a plan they can follow. Ask us about the clinic referral share.
